\subsection{Context components and weight sensitivity}
Each state or context-group squared distance is divided by its source-library mean off-diagonal pair distance. The contextual distance equally averages equipment, score and utility groups. The fixed mixture is $d_\lambda=(1-\lambda)d_{\mathrm{state}}+\lambda d_{\mathrm{context}}$, with $\lambda=0.5$ and prespecified sensitivities 0.25 and 0.75. Single-group and context-only controls are retained. Unknown source context makes that condition unsupported; an unknown query is not assigned a zero distance. No encoder weights are updated. % [claim:C151] [evidence:E121]

\label{app:contextpolicies}
Single-group controls and the prespecified weights probe different context contributions. They are separated from the core graph/nongraph and static/temporal comparisons.

We retain the frozen state encoders and add explicit CT context: five log equipment totals, two public pre-round scores, and thirty log throw counts over the five times and six grenade types, for 37 coordinates. Equipment reweights information already present in the node input; score and recorded utility supply additional observations. Opponent equipment and raw view angles are excluded. % [claim:C150] [evidence:E121]

\subsection{Existing judged-pool replay}
\label{app:oldpool}
The old-pair replay uses graded discounted gain, with cutoff $\min(5,N)$ and the ideal ranking restricted to the same eligible rated pool. Its discounted-gain definition is
\begin{equation}
 \mathrm{DCG@\min(5,N)}=\sum_{k=1}^{\min(5,N)}
 \frac{2^{r_k}-1}{\log_2(k+1)}.
\end{equation}
Grades for an exact pair are averaged before ranking evaluation; missing or unjudgeable values are not zero. Singleton and all-zero-gain pools are undefined. Seeds are averaged within query before query macro-averaging. These candidate-pool scores do not establish full-library recall or new-series relevance.

Table~\ref{tab:oldpool} ranks only the eligible rated candidates for each old query, with gains $2^g-1$ and cutoff $\min(5,N)$. Neural seeds are averaged within each query before the query macro-average. The old materials omitted the new utility/score facets, candidate selection depended on earlier methods, and grader independence was not verified. This is an information-limited development observation, not a new independent tactical test or full-library retrieval metric. % [claim:C156] [evidence:E125,E126]

\subsection{Sampling precision}
The technical-confirmation planning scenario balances six maps with two series per map. Under independent normal within-map log-ratio residuals with SD 0.05, a 12-series design has approximately 93.9\% probability that the nominal 95\% interval half-width is at most 0.05 log units; SD 0.10 requires 24 series for 80\% width assurance under the same model. These are assumption-dependent precision calculations, not tactical-effect power or proof that 12 series suffice for the full study. The new cohort and source checkpoints were locally frozen before archive acquisition; its technical results are reported below. % [claim:C157] [evidence:E127,E129]

\section{Telemetry Compatibility}
\label{app:telemetry}
Native export on the new cohort preserves the same fifteen node-feature channels and three pair-feature channels and yields finite, mask-consistent inputs. This verifies computational availability, not tactical semantics. Dedicated throw events are not exposed for the four May series; subsequent event-stage adapters recover qualified release observations while preserving unresolved counts as unknown. Pre-round scores and CT equipment are available for all selected windows. % [claim:C188] [evidence:E130,E135,E150]

A version-sensitivity probe reparsed one unchanged May Dust2 Demo with demoparser2 0.42.1 instead of 0.41.3. The dedicated throw event remained unavailable, while opening snapshots, timing audits and all five model-input arrays matched exactly. This single-file result does not determine whether recording differences or a remaining compatibility issue causes the event gap, and it does not validate other parser-version substitutions. % [claim:C189] [evidence:E138]

On one October Dust2 control, all 535 paired dedicated releases occur 6--14 game ticks after firing and exactly at the first observed projectile tick; firing timestamps are not interchangeable with releases. After the conservative correction, all 330,660 candidate positions supported in the preceding context version and 4,766 previously known prototype assignments retain their candidate identities or cluster labels and acceptance, with distances checked to absolute tolerance $10^{-10}$. These are telemetry and replay checks, not semantic judgments. % [claim:C200] [evidence:E139,E153]

Native fields distinguish firing from completed release: in one unmatched T-side Molotov action, a positive planned throw time follows pin release, but the owner dies before that time, outside all query windows. Across eight dedicated-event controls, 3,553 normal releases and 830 CT query counts match. Two smoke projectiles instead appear at owner death without a started release or dedicated throw event. These smoke counterexamples are not extrapolated to the unmatched target HE projectile, whose affected counts remain unknown. % [claim:C221] [evidence:E149,E150]

Pre-round score and CT equipment are known for all 246 windows. Corrective telemetry adapters recover 79 of the 80 originally unavailable May windows, raising complete utility support from 166 to 245. One Inferno window retains unknown HE death-drop semantics at 12, 15, 18 and 20 seconds, with its 10-second count known. All original policies and controls are retained; missing counts are not zero-imputed. The native event-stage audit is described in Supplementary Appendix~D. % [claim:C226] [evidence:E150,E151]
